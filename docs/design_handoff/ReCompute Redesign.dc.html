NEW · DARK MODE, MENU, SETTINGS

Dark theme, a menu button and a settings page.

The ☰ button sits at the top right of every page. It lists every page of the app and the account: profile, Settings and Log out (or Technician login for visitors). Settings has three parts: Profile, Credentials and Appearance (Light, Dark, Match device). The choice is saved on the device and applies to every page.

Try it: open ☰ on any artboard, or pick Dark in the live Settings artboards (N2). Every artboard not marked “forced” switches, including section 03 below.

RECOMPUTE · REDESIGN v2 · TECHNICAL DIRECTION

An instrument panel for a device's second life.

The app should read like infrastructure documentation: precise, plain and checkable. Monospace carries the machine facts (IDs, hashes, timestamps, readings); a system sans carries the explanations. Hairline grids, square corners and one ink-blue accent keep it calm. The three-block hash chain is the only motif, used in the logo and the passport timeline, so nothing relies on eco imagery.

HOW THE 10-SECOND STORY LANDS

  1. Home leads with one sentence and three numbered steps: passport → decision → second life.
  2. Each listing card answers outcome, price and passport integrity above its fold.
  3. The passport page opens with the device ID, outcome and integrity badge at phone size; the For sale box is the second block.
  4. SAMPLE DATA sits in the dark utility bar on every page, in amber that reads as a label, not a warning.
